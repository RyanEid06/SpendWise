## Engineering history

The `WP35_*`, `WP36_*`, `WP37_*`, `WP38_*` documents, `SpendWise_WP35_Implementation_Plan.md` and `superpowers/plans/` preserve dated specifications, investigations, reviews and delivery receipts. Their original branch names, old versions, failures and pending steps describe those checkpoints; they do not override the current status or reopen completed work.

For the final release, use the [WP38 report](WP38_REPORT.md), [execution ledger](WP38_PROGRESS.md), [accepted test APK receipt](WP38_PRIVATE_APK.md) and [public release receipt](WP38_PUBLIC_RELEASE.md). Older reports remain available for traceability rather than being rewritten as current acceptance evidence.

Generated recordings, logs and APK copies are deliberately excluded from Git. Local evidence paths in historical reports may refer to retained archives; they are not prerequisites for cloning, building or browsing this repository.
